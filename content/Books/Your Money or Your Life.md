My takeaways
- Learn to think of your expenses in terms of life energy. For example, if I buy this coffee for $5, how long do I have to work to pay it off? Same thing goes for a car, a bike, a house. 
- This is a fundamental book for the FIRE community
- Basically if have a savings rate of 50% for 15 years, I'm retired. 
- Learning to live below my means is the clearest path to real freedom
